AN INVITATION TO REMEMBER

A moment in history. A message for today.

Reformation Day, observed on October 31, traditionally commemorates Martin Luther's 95 Theses of 1517 and the beginning of the Protestant Reformation.

We gather to remember that history, reflect on God's Word, and celebrate the Gospel of Jesus Christ together.
